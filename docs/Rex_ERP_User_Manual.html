CRM & Commercial Management

The CRM module manages the complete pre-sales funnel: Leads qualification, Deals pipeline, Contacts, Follow-up reminders, Quotations, and Sales Orders.

1. Leads & Customer Registry

Captures corporate names, BRN/TIN tax numbers, primary contact persons, credit limits, and billing addresses. Prevents transactions with unverified accounts.

2. Product vs Custom Quotes

Product Quotations use catalog units. Custom Engineering Quotes use profile length, width, and height (mm) to compute precise aluminum extrusion costs.

3. Multi-Tier Approvals

Quotations with profit margins below policy thresholds (e.g. < 20%) or customer credit limits are routed to management for formal sign-off before dispatch.

Attention Contact Person (attn) System Enforcement

The Attention Person (attn) specifies the authorized corporate officer responsible for the contract. This field is mandatory on Sales Orders and cascades through all downstream documents.

Downstream Document Attention String Handling Operational & Legal Consequence
Sales Order (SO-XXXX) Captured & Stored in Database Locks individual customer authority; printed on customer order confirmation.
Advance Receipt (REC-XXXX) Inherited on Left & Right Panels Printed on official A4 voucher: "Received with thanks from ... Attn: [Officer]".
Gate Pass (GP-XXXX) Pre-filled in Delivery Note Security checkpoint verifies recipient contact before goods exit the factory.
Tax Invoice (INV-XXXX) Displayed on Tax Invoice Header Ensures commercial tax invoice is delivered directly to the designated officer.

Inventory Management & Cut Optimization

The Inventory module maintains real-time valuation of raw profiles, hardware accessories, and finished assemblies across central and branch storage yards.

Stock Ledger Audit Types

Tracks all movements: IN (GRN receipts), OUT / DISPATCH (Sales deliveries), SCRAP (Offcut waste), and ADJUSTMENT (Stocktaking audits).

Linear Stock Cut Studio

Calculates nesting layouts on 6,000mm standard aluminum extrusions. Maximizes profile yields, accounts for 3mm blade kerf, and tags reusable off-cuts (> 1,200mm).

Material Issue Hub

Enforces authorized requisition slips before materials leave raw stores. Deducts available inventory and transfers value into Work-in-Progress (WIP).

Security Gate Pass System (Goods Delivery vs Sample)

Security checkpoints control factory perimeter exits using dual gate pass formats:

1. Commercial Goods Gate Pass

Issued upon confirmed Sales Order dispatch. Cross-references commercial invoices and customer delivery receipts.

2. Sample Outward Gate Pass

Issued for client mockups, architect samples, or warranty inspections. Requires scheduled return dates and custodian sign-off.

Production Execution & Machinery Management

Work Orders progress through structured fabrication stages: Profile Cutting, CNC Milling, Surface Finishing, Unit Assembly, Glazing, and Final Quality Control (QC).

  • Plant & Machinery Hub: Logs machine tools, CNC cutters, and anodizing baths to monitor operating hours, prevent breakdown stoppages, and compute asset depreciation.
  • Quality Control Inspection: Completed units are tagged with serial barcode stickers before being transferred into the Finished Goods bay.

Commercial Dispatch & Non-Mandatory Vehicle Rule

When products are packaged and ready for delivery, the warehouse opens the Commercial Dispatch Modal.

Logistics SOP
Dispatch Resolution: Vehicle Number is Non-Mandatory
To prevent shipping delays when clients collect products via foot, three-wheeler, or third-party courier without a known license plate, the Vehicle Number field in the dispatch dialog is strictly Optional. Dispatches submit without validation errors when the vehicle number is left empty.
