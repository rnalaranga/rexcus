3. Double-Entry Accounting Golden Rules (හර / බැර මූලධර්ම)

1. Real Accounts (Assets / වත්කම්)
ලැබෙන දේ හර (Debit), පිටවන දේ බැර (Credit)
Rule: Debit what comes in, Credit what goes out.
Examples: Cash/Bank received is Debit (Dr). Cash/Bank paid out is Credit (Cr). Machinery purchased is Debit (Dr).
2. Personal Accounts (Debtors/Creditors)
ලබන්නා හර (Debit), ලබාදෙන්නා බැර (Credit)
Rule: Debit the receiver, Credit the giver.
Examples: Credit customer balance is Debit (Dr AR). Material purchased on credit is Credit (Cr AP) to supplier.
3. Nominal Accounts (Revenue/Expenses)
සියලු වියදම් හර (Debit), සියලු ආදායම් බැර (Credit)
Rule: Debit all expenses & losses, Credit all income & gains.
Examples: Electricity, machine repairs, wages are Debit (Dr Expense). Precision sales revenue is Credit (Cr Sales).

6. Sri Lanka Taxation Audit (VAT 18% & SSCL 2.5%)

The Inland Revenue Department requires compounding calculation for VAT and SSCL. The ERP engine calculation is verified as follows:

1. Base Amount = Net Goods Value after discount
2. SSCL (2.5%) = Base Amount × 0.025               [Posted to GL: 2220]
3. VAT (18%)   = (Base Amount + SSCL) × 0.18         [Posted to GL: 2210]
4. Gross Total = Base Amount + SSCL + VAT             [Effective Tax Rate: 20.95%]
